证专业认证Professional certifications

Credentials that turn intent into accountability.

Issued through the Chartered ESG Institute — the Foundation's SDG 9 initiative for industry, innovation and infrastructure. Six designations recognise the professionals and organisations delivering real ESG and sustainability outcomes. Sample certificates are shown below.

View sample ↗
CCA

Chartered Carbon Accountant™

Carbon accounting and emissions reporting — carbon inventory analysis, ISO 14064 / 14065 compliance, and corporate carbon-footprint assessment. Supports organisations working toward carbon neutrality.

View sample ↗
CLA

ESG Lead Auditor™

Conducts audits for sustainability certifications and validates ESG compliance — ISO standards expertise, risk assessment, and ESG reporting & assurance.

View sample ↗
AT

Accredited ESG Trainer™

Trains and certifies professionals in ESG standards, frameworks and best practice — carbon accounting, ESG reporting, and candidate preparation.

View sample ↗
SPM

Sustainability Project Manager™

Oversees sustainability initiatives aligned with the UN SDGs — strategy implementation, carbon-project validation, and stakeholder engagement.

View sample ↗
ECM

ESG Corporate Membership™

For corporates making genuine efforts on sustainability projects. Company directors trained in ESG, SDG and sustainability; collaboration on ESG, SDG, sustainability and green-technology projects.

View sample ↗
CEE

Carbon Equivalents Executive™

Carbon-equivalency frameworks and sustainable resource management — leadership guidance, sustainable supply-chain optimisation, and executive ESG strategy.

Certificates shown are samples marked confidential; issued designations carry the holder's name and a verifiable credential ID. Learn more or verify a certificate at the Chartered ESG Institute.
