使使命Our mission

Where government and enterprise leave a gap, we build the bridge.

The private sector is good at building products and services. The public sector is good at delivering solutions to people and industries. Between the two lies a gap — and that is where the Foundation works.

We spur innovation to improve the human condition by stepping in where governments and business leave gaps, creating market incentives and delivering tools to those most in need. We give enterprising people a friendly platform to communicate, propose ideas, and grow — a community committed to finding real solutions to global problems and helping the world deliver on the Sustainable Development Goals.

What the Foundation is

  • 聚A society for interchange and development — advancing the ecosystem, science, and craft of enterprise.
  • 建A strategic advisory community of entrepreneurs and thought leaders working on global problems.
  • 通A bridge to governments, semi-government bodies, and multilateral institutions on the SDGs.
  • 养A place that gives young entrepreneurs the holistic guidance to grow, personally and professionally.
架架构The 108 architecture
九 × 十二 = 一〇八

One number holds the whole.

The Foundation is rooted in the Chinese almanac. The Book of Changes gives nine — the fullness of the yang — and the twelve earthly branches complete the circuit of the heavens. Nine times twelve is 108: the exact count of our national and city representations, arranged in three tiers.
